# [2.3] Notification emails and weekly digest: Opus

- **In-app notifications** are written straight away (`core/notify.ts`, from 2.4).
- **Email fan-out:** a cron job every 15 minutes sweeps notifications that haven't been emailed (`email/deliver.ts`).
  - It sends the ones the user wants by email. **Defaults:** messages, collab requests and system notices are on; everything else is off until the user turns it on in Settings.
  - It skips notifications already read in the app.
  - On a failed send it retries on the next sweep.
  - Old notifications are never emailed retroactively.
- **Weekly digest:** Mondays at 15:00 UTC, for users with `weeklyDigest` on who had news that week, and never more than once a week.
- **Providers** (`email/send.ts`), tried in this order: a Cloudflare Email Service `EMAIL` binding (needs the Workers Paid plan), then the `RESEND_API_KEY` secret (Resend's free tier), then nothing. With no provider, emails are skipped and in-app notifications still work.
- **Magic-link sign-in** now emails the link when a provider is configured.
- **Templates:** the built-ins in `email/render.ts` escape all user text. Flash's designed templates (2.13) will replace them.
- **Migration `0003`:** `notifications.emailed_at` and `users.digest_sent_at`.
- **New vars:** `SITE_ORIGIN` (local, preview, production); optional `EMAIL_FROM`.

**Owner decision:** pick an email provider.
- **Free:** create a Resend account, verify `forklift.stream` there, then run `npx wrangler secret put RESEND_API_KEY --env preview`.
- **Paid:** upgrade to Workers Paid ($5/month), onboard `forklift.stream` in Email Service, and Opus adds the `send_email` binding.

Tests: `test/integration/email.test.ts`.
