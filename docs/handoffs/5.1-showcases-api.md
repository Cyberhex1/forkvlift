# 5.1 Showcases API (Opus)

The server side of showcases is in. Sol builds the feed, page and composer (5.4–5.6) and Flash the badges and copy (5.7–5.8) on top of it.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-10)

- Showcases may link paid or commercial apps. They are plain links; nothing about placement can be bought.
- Videos can be uploaded (the existing `media` upload kind: mp4/webm, 25 MB cap) **or** embedded from YouTube/Vimeo. Embeds are normalized to `youtube-nocookie.com` / `player.vimeo.com` player URLs; other hosts are refused.

## Contract (`packages/shared`)

- Schemas: `ShowcaseCard`, `ShowcaseDetail`, `ShowcaseInput`, `ShowcaseComment`, `ShowcaseKind` (web, mobile, desktop, game, art, video, tool, other), `ShowcaseLinkKind` (live, app_store, play_store, download, demo, repo, store, other).
- Routes:
  - `listShowcases` (sort trending/new/top; filters category, kind, tool, q, staffPicks), `getShowcase`
  - `createShowcase` (Turnstile, `publish` true/false) and `updateShowcase` (`publish: true` publishes a draft, `false` unpublishes), `deleteShowcase`
  - `myShowcases` (drafts included), `savedShowcases`, `userShowcases`
  - `voteShowcase`, `saveShowcase`
  - `listShowcaseComments`, `createShowcaseComment` (Turnstile), `deleteShowcaseComment` (author, the maker, or an admin)
  - Admin: `setShowcaseStaffPick`
- Reports: new targets `showcase` and `showcase_comment`. Moderators can hide or delete them from the queue.

## Rules the UI should follow

- **Labels:** always show a "Showcase" badge, plus "Closed source" when `hasPublicCode` is false. Never show clone, fork or "Continue this".
- **Catalog link:** when `catalogProject` is set (its repo is listed in the catalog), link "See the code" to `/p/<slug>`.
- **Download links:** links with kind `download` get the "review before running" note.
- **Media:** upload first (`createUpload` kind `media` for images, GIFs and video; `thumbnail` also works for the cover), then send keys. The cover must be an image. Up to 12 media items and 8 links.
- **Pages:** the showcase page lives at `/showcase/<slug>` (notification links use it).
- **Limits:** you can't upvote your own showcase, and new showcases are limited to 10 per account per day.
